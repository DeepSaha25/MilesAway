About MilesAway

MilesAway is a running and fitness tracking app for outdoor runners. The app helps users track GPS-based runs, view distance, pace, calories, route history, streaks, weekly progress, leaderboards, and community activity.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, MilesAway may collect the following information:

  • Account information: name, email address, password/authentication credentials, and account timestamps.
  • Profile information: avatar, weight, timezone, total distance, running streak, and app profile details.
  • Location information: coarse and precise location, GPS route coordinates, latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, speed, heading, timestamps, and approximate city, district, state, or country derived from your location.
  • Run and fitness information: distance, duration, pace, speed, calories burned, elevation gain, start time, end time, run date, route history, and run summaries.
  • Community information: posts, comments, likes, and related activity inside community features.
  • Technical information: authentication tokens, API request data, error logs, device/app interaction data, and information needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Track outdoor runs and display your live route, distance, pace, calories, and progress.
  • Save run history and show performance statistics, weekly progress, streaks, and leaderboards.
  • Enable community features such as posts, comments, and likes.
  • Maintain app security, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve app performance.
  • Respond to support requests and account-related questions.

Location Data

MilesAway requests location permission so it can track outdoor runs, show your route, calculate run metrics, and update location-based profile or leaderboard features. Location tracking is used while you are actively using run tracking features. If you deny location permission, GPS run tracking may not work.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only when needed to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and safety, or use infrastructure providers that help us host, store, secure, and deliver the service.

Data Retention

We keep account, run, profile, and community information for as long as needed to provide MilesAway services, maintain user history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. You can request deletion of your account or personal information. For detailed instructions on how to do this, please visit our Delete Account page.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. Passwords are stored in hashed form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can choose not to provide optional profile details.
  • You can disable location permission from your device settings, but GPS tracking features may stop working.
  • You can request deletion of your personal information via our dedicated Delete Account page or by contacting us.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.

Children's Privacy

MilesAway is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we update it, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of MilesAway after an update means you accept the revised policy.
