How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In the MilesAway App (Recommended)

You can delete your account and associated data directly within the mobile application:

  • Open the **MilesAway** app on your phone.
  • Navigate to your **Profile** screen.
  • Scroll down to the **ACCOUNT & DATA** section.
  • Tap **Delete account**.
  • Enter your current password in the confirmation dialog and tap **Delete permanently**.

Option 2: Direct Email Request

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, you can request manual deletion via email. Simply contact our support team:

📧 Email: ideepsaha25@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address registered to your MilesAway account. Include your full name or username to help us process it quickly.

Request Data Deletion via Email

What Happens to Your Data?

When you request account deletion, all data tied to your profile is permanently purged from our databases. This is an irreversible process and includes:

  • Your profile information, settings, and login credentials.
  • Your complete run history, GPS routes, metrics, and achievements.
  • Your shared community posts, messages, and social media feed history.
  • Your ranking data, leaderboards entries, and personal records.

Please note that data deletion requests sent via email are typically processed within **48 to 72 hours**.
